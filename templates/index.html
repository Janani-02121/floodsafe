FloodSafe

Smart Flood Evacuation Route Finder

FloodSafe helps people find the safest and shortest evacuation route to a nearby shelter during floods. It uses Dijkstra's Algorithm to calculate routes while avoiding blocked roads and minimizing flood risk.

Find a Safe Route

How It Works

📍 Choose Your Location

Select your current location on the interactive map.

🏠 Pick a Shelter

Choose the nearest safe shelter as your destination.

🧮 Dijkstra Calculates

Our own Dijkstra implementation finds the lowest-cost path, avoiding blocked and heavily flooded roads.

🗺️ Get Your Route

View the safest route on the map with distance and estimated time.
